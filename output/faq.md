## How much storage is included in each plan?

The Standard plan includes 2TB of storage per user, while the Enterprise plan offers unlimited storage.

## What encryption methods does Acme Cloud Storage use?

Files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

## What is the pricing for Acme Cloud Storage plans?

The Standard plan costs $8 per user per month (billed annually), and the Enterprise plan costs $22 per user per month.

## Is there a free trial available?

Yes, a 14-day free trial is available for both Standard and Enterprise plans, with no credit card required.

## How long is file version history retained?

File version history is retained for 30 days on the Standard plan and 180 days on the Enterprise plan.

## What support options are available?

Enterprise customers receive 24/7 live chat and email support. Standard plan customers get email support with responses within one business day, Monday through Friday, 9am–6pm Eastern.

## What integrations are available with Acme Cloud Storage?

Acme Cloud Storage integrates with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, and Zapier. An API is also available for custom integrations.

## Can I choose where my data is stored?

Enterprise plan customers can choose their preferred data residency region (US, EU, or Asia-Pacific) at account setup. Changing the region after account creation requires opening a support ticket.

## Is two-factor authentication (2FA) supported?

Yes, 2FA is available for all accounts and mandatory for Enterprise accounts. Supported methods include authenticator apps (TOTP) and SMS codes.

## What happens to deleted files?

Deleted files go to a recycle bin and are permanently removed after 30 days unless restored.

## Who will help me during my first days at Acme Corp?

Your manager will assign you an onboarding buddy on day one to help answer questions.

## What should I do if my laptop doesn't arrive on my first day?

If your laptop hasn't arrived by 9am on day one, contact it-support@acmecorp.example.

## When is my company email account created and how do I get the password?

Your company email account is created automatically the night before your start date, and your temporary password is sent via text message to the phone number on file.

## When does health insurance coverage begin?

Health insurance coverage begins on your first day of employment, with no waiting period.

## How much does Acme Corp contribute to health insurance premiums?

Acme Corp covers 100% of the employee premium and 70% of dependent premiums for the base medical plan.

## How much PTO do full-time employees receive?

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year during their first two years, increasing to 20 days starting in year three.

## Is there a waiting period before I can use my PTO?

No, PTO accrues monthly and can be used as soon as it is earned; there is no waiting period.

## How much unused PTO can I carry over to the next year?

Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited on December 31st.

## How long is the probation period for new hires?

All new hires undergo a 90-day probation period with formal check-ins at the 30-day and 60-day marks, and a final review at day 90.

## What is the notice requirement during the probation period?

Either party may end employment during probation with two weeks' notice.

## What is the return window for items?

Items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. Items returned between day 31 and day 60 are eligible for store credit only at 100% of the purchase price. No returns are accepted after 60 days.

## What condition must items be in to qualify for a return?

Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals. Items showing signs of use may be subject to a 15% restocking fee, which will be deducted from the refund.

## How do I start a return?

Log into your account, go to Order History, and select Start a Return next to the relevant order. You will receive a prepaid shipping label by email within 24 hours. Drop the package off at any authorized carrier location within 10 days of receiving the label.

## How long does it take to receive a refund?

Refunds are processed within 5 business days of the returned item arriving at the warehouse, and typically appear on your original payment method within 3-7 additional business days, depending on your bank.

## What items cannot be returned?

Gift cards, digital downloads, and clearance items marked Final Sale cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances.

## Are exchanges available?

Direct exchanges are only available for size or color variants of the same product, and only while supplies last. If the requested variant is unavailable, the original item will be refunded instead.

## Is there a restocking fee for returned items?

Items showing signs of use may be subject to a 15% restocking fee, which will be deducted from the refund. Unused items in original packaging are not subject to a restocking fee.

## What happens if I return an item between day 31 and day 60?

Items returned between day 31 and day 60 are eligible for store credit only, at 100% of the purchase price. Full refunds are only available for returns within 30 days of delivery.
